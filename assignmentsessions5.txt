Que-1. Create a simple IPL Fan Bot that takes your favorite IPL team name as input and uses if-else-if statements to print a unique cheer message for each team (e.g., 'Go Mumbai Indians!', 'Chennai Super Kings for the win!'). If the team is not recognized, print 'Team not found!'

        printf("This is New Delhi! Go DC! 💙");

        printf("Halla Bol! Go Rajasthan Royals! 💗");
